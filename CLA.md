is merged, you must accept this Contributor License Agreement ("CLA") by

acceptance in your first pull request.

1. The contribution is your original work, OR you have sufficient rights to

3. You will identify any third-party licenses or restrictions that apply to

## How to accept

1. Configure git to sign-off your commits: `git config user.name`, `git
   config user.email`, then `git commit -s` (or `--signoff`).
2. In your first pull request, add a comment stating: "I have read and accept
   the Preston-Check CLA."

That's it. You only need to do this once. Subsequent contributions are
covered by the same acceptance.

## Why this is necessary

Open-source projects with commercial sustainability need the right to
relicense contributions to support the commercial product that funds
maintenance. Without a CLA, every contribution would have to be tracked
separately, and any future license decision (such as adding a feature to
the audit-package layer) would require finding and negotiating with every
past contributor. This CLA solves that problem while preserving your
copyright and your right to use your own contribution however you wish
elsewhere.
